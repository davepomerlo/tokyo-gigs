Chanson Française 15: To Live Unofficial

A Chanson Show Featuring Classical Singers

2026/10/19(mo.) 19:00 Starting

Hareza Ikebukuro, Toshima Community Center, Small Hall （Tokyo）

Official https://eplus.jp/sf/detail/4548520001

Japanese Page >

The theme of “Chanson Française 15” is “Life.” In just three minutes, we present the world of chanson—literary, high-quality, and theatrical—that candidly portrays the full spectrum of the human condition, including love, loneliness, betrayal, jealousy, aging, and death. For this performance, under the direction of pianist Tomoko Tanaka—who honed her craft in chanson clubs—eight accomplished classical singers, active at the forefront of opera and musical theater, have come together. Unamplified, live vocals combined with an exquisite ensemble of piano and violin will bring these carefully selected masterpieces—which weave together the subtleties of life—to vivid life. Please enjoy to your heart’s content this performance of chanson as a performing art, where an intense drama unfolds that transcends the boundaries of music.
