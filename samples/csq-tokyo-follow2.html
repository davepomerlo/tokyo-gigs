Let's Sing About Fall | "Sing-Along Café" Featuring Live Performances by Professional Musicians

Sing-Along Café: Fall 2026

2026/10/20(Tu.) 13:30 Starting

Nakao Construction Industries, 1st Floor Showroom: Café Smile （Tokyo）

Official https://www.nakaokensetu.co.jp/cafe/v2data/page/cont397.html

Japanese Page >

We’re hosting an autumn-themed sing-along café at ShowRoom Café Smile in Tokumaru, Itabashi Ward. Leading the singing will be Mo-chan (MO-TOY), who is also well-known as our resident opera singer. Accompanied by a live performance featuring two flutes and piano by the Kano Flute Ensemble, we’ll enjoy nostalgic songs from our childhood and other memorable tunes such as “Autumn in the Village,” “Red Dragonfly,” “Autumn Leaves,” and “Cosmos,” among other nostalgic and memorable songs we sang as children. You don’t have to be a great singer—whether you sing at the top of your lungs, hum along, or simply listen, everyone is welcome to enjoy this warm, musical gathering. Relax and unwind with a cup of coffee or tea.

Program

  • Autumn in the Village

  • Village Festival

  • The Tanuki Drumming at Shōjō-ji Temple

  • Red Dragonfly

  • Autumn Foliage

  • A Good Day to Set Out on a Journey

  • Falling in Love
